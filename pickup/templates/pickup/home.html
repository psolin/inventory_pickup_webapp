When Do I Use This?

Use this app whenever customer items are moved to or from the "Sold" holding area.

How Do I Do It?

Tap the button at the bottom-right of the screen to add a new transaction and its items. The transaction will appear under Active. Once all items are picked up, the transaction moves to Archived.

Pages
  • Home — Dashboard with stats and this help section.
  • Active — Transactions still awaiting pickup.
  • Archived — Completed and forfeited transactions.
What If I Have Issues?
